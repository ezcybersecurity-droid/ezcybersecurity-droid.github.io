Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

The EZ CyberSec Analytics app is a private tool used only by the owner of the EZ CyberSec YouTube channels.

  • It only reads YouTube and YouTube Analytics data for channels the owner connects.
  • Access is read-only. It cannot upload, edit, or delete anything.
  • The data is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to review the channel's performance.
  • No data is shared with, or sold to, anyone.

To remove the app's access, go to your Google Account, then Security, then Third-party apps with account access.
